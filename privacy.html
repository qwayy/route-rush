PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Route Rush KC app for Android, the browser version at qwayy.github.io/route-rush, and this website. Route Rush KC is made by Qwaysar Productions ("we", "us").

The short version: we don't run any servers, there are no accounts, and your game progress stays on your device. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them. The browser version has no ads.

What stays on your device

Your scores, progress, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved on your device only (in the app's storage, or in your browser's storage for the web version). We never receive them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes them.

Tilt steering reads your phone's motion sensors while you play. These readings are used on the device to steer and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Ads (Android app only)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you can watch for a reward. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • App activity, such as app launches and taps on ads
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

Google handles this data under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive this data, only overall reports like how many ads were shown.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your answer any time with PRIVACY OPTIONS on the title screen. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).

Purchases

If you buy something in the app (such as Remove Ads or coin packs), the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google Play only tells the app what you bought so it can unlock it. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payments.

Feedback emails

The FEEDBACK button opens your own email app with a message filled in: your rating and comments, plus details that help us fix problems (your device or browser type, screen size, frame rate, how far you got in the game and your game settings). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email. If you do, we get your email address and the message, use them only to answer you and improve the game, and delete them if you ask.

This website

This website and the browser version of the game are hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or trackers here. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Route Rush KC is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example in a feedback email), contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

Because your game data lives only on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing the site data in your browser. To have a feedback email deleted, or for any privacy question, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
