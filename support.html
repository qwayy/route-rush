Common questions

TILT STEERING PULLS TO ONE SIDE

Hold the phone the way you like to play, pause the game and tap RECENTER TILT. That grip becomes "straight". You can also change the tilt sensitivity on the title screen, or steer by dragging your finger instead.

HOW DO I THROW?

Tap the left half of the screen to throw left and the right half to throw right. On a computer, use A and D. You can replay the tutorial any time with REPLAY TUTORIAL on the title screen.

I BOUGHT SOMETHING BUT DON'T HAVE IT

Tap RESTORE PURCHASES on the title screen while signed in to the same Google account you bought with. If it's still missing, email us with the date of purchase and your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

WILL I LOSE MY PROGRESS?

Progress is saved on your device and survives app updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it, and progress can't be moved between phones. The browser version keeps its own separate save.

CAN I TURN OFF ADS?

Ads between runs can be removed with the Remove Ads purchase. The optional reward ads (like Keep Riding or Double Coins) are always your choice. To change your ad privacy choices, tap PRIVACY OPTIONS on the title screen if it's shown in your region.

THE GAME RUNS SLOWLY

Tap the graphics button (🖥) on the title screen and choose a lower setting. Closing other apps can help too.
