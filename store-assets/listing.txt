APP NAME (max 30)
Route Rush KC

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Ride Kansas City streets, toss papers, smash mailboxes. One more route!

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
The papers are bundled, your bike is oiled, and Kansas City is waiting. Grab your satchel and RUSH!

Route Rush KC is a pixel-art paper-route game. Steer down busy streets, toss newspapers onto porches, and nail mailboxes for big points. Keep every subscriber happy and the Mayor might just pin a medal on you.

★ RIDE THE REAL KC
A new neighborhood every day of the week:
• Monday: Waldo's quiet bungalow blocks
• Tuesday: Brookside, with Tudor houses, big fall trees and morning joggers
• Wednesday: the Plaza, with Spanish-style towers, fountains and shoppers
• Thursday: Westport's storefronts and busier traffic
• Friday: 18th & Vine, with brick blocks and jazz murals
• Saturday: the River Market, with lofts and the streetcar (ding ding!)
• Sunday: Game Day! Red and gold tailgates, crowds and the hardest route of the week

★ EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
• Tilt your phone to steer (or drag your finger)
• Tap the left or right side to throw
• Porch = points. Mailbox = BIG points.
• Houses without the marker didn't pay? Break a window for a bonus
• Dodge dogs, cars, potholes, skaters and more

★ BONUS COURSE
Finish your route and hit the bonus course: jump ramps, smack targets and rack up extra points before the day is done.

★ PERFECT DAYS & COMBOS
Deliver to every subscriber for a PERFECT DELIVERY score multiplier. Chain hits to build your combo.

★ POWER-UPS
Grab Double Points, the Paper Magnet, Slow-Mo, a Shield or a full-on Paper Storm right off the road.

★ RIDERS WITH SKILLS
Seven riders, each with their own perk: Sure Shot, Quick Arm, Smooth Rider, Dog Whisperer, Combo King, Lucky Charm and Hustler. Unlock new riders as you play.

★ BUILD YOUR GARAGE
Earn bikes with real perks: BMX, Cruiser, Road Bike, Fat Tire and E-Bike. Then show off with paint jobs and outfits.

★ MEDALS & STORY MOMENTS
Finish a full week and head downtown for a ceremony. Earn bronze, silver, the Key to the City, and a gold-medal parade. The route keeps going week after week, so how far can you ride?

★ A DAILY SPIN
Spin the wheel once a day for coins and cosmetic prizes.

Short runs, quick restarts, chunky pixel art and a big love letter to Kansas City. Ready to ride?

Route Rush KC is free to play and contains ads.
