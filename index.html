HOW TO PLAY

STEER

Tilt your phone, drag your finger, or use the arrow keys on a computer.

THROW

Tap the left or right side of the screen (A / D on a keyboard). Your papers never run out.

SCORE

Porch 300. Mailbox 700. Deliver to every subscriber for a Perfect Delivery bonus.
